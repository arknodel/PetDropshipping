## Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United States only, including APO/FPO addresses where the carrier allows. We do not ship internationally at this time.

## Processing time

Orders are processed within **1–3 business days** (Monday–Friday, excluding US holidays). Orders placed after `[[cutoff time, e.g. 12 pm ET]]` start processing the next business day.

## Delivery time

Each product page shows its own delivery estimate. Estimates start **after** processing:

| Ships from | Estimated delivery after processing |
|---|---|
| US warehouse | 2–7 days, depending on the item |
| International warehouse (China) | 5–11 days |

These are carrier estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. Weather, carrier volume and address issues can cause delays.

## Orders with more than one item

Items in one order may ship from different warehouses, in separate packages, on different days. You will receive tracking for each package.

## Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a tracking number. Tracking can take 24–48 hours to show movement.

## Lost, late or damaged packages

- **Late:** if your package has not arrived 10 days after the latest estimated date on your order, email us and we will investigate with the carrier.
- **Marked delivered but not received:** check with neighbors and your local post office, then email us within 7 days of the delivery scan.
- **Damaged in transit:** email us photos within 30 days of delivery. See our Returns & Refunds policy.

## Address changes

Email us within 12 hours of placing your order to change the shipping address. After an order is processed we may not be able to change it. We are not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect address entered at checkout, but we will help where we can.
