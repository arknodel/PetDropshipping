This policy explains how `[[legal business name]]` ("Fondly Pet", "we") collects, uses and shares personal information when you visit fondlypet.com or shop.fondlypet.com, or buy from us.

## What we collect

- **Order information:** name, email, phone (optional), shipping and billing address, and the items you buy.
- **Payment information:** handled by our checkout and payment providers (Shopify and its payment processors). We do not see or store your full card number.
- **Messages:** what you send us by email or our contact form.
- **Device and usage information:** IP address, browser type, pages viewed and similar data, collected through cookies and similar technologies by our store platform and website host.

## How we use it

- To process, ship and track your order, and to handle returns and refunds
- To answer your questions and provide support
- To prevent fraud and keep the store secure
- To send marketing emails **only if you opt in**. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To understand how the site is used so we can improve it

## Who we share it with

We share only what each partner needs to do its job:

- **Shopify:** our store and checkout platform
- **Payment processors:** to take payment
- **CJdropshipping:** our fulfillment partner. It receives your name, shipping address, phone (if given) and order items so it can pack and ship your order.
- **Shipping carriers:** to deliver your package
- **Cloudflare:** hosts our website and protects it from attacks
- Authorities, when the law requires it

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. `[[If ad pixels (e.g. Meta, Google) are added later, update this section and the cookie notice before they go live.]]`

## Cookies

Our store platform uses cookies that are needed for the cart and checkout, plus analytics cookies. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for orders, returns, taxes and accounting, typically `[[7 years]]`. We delete or anonymize other data when it is no longer needed.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, Colorado or other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. To make a request, email `[[privacy/support email]]`. We will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

## Children

Our store is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
